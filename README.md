# The Henry and Palfrey Family

Nicole’s mother’s side: Joy Henry, her sister Marilyn and brother Andy, and their parents Al Henry and Priscilla Palfrey Henry. Includes the Moncton, New Brunswick, years as family origin and as city history, with the same confidence labels throughout.

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Published at https://shea-nelson.github.io/nicole-henry-family-history/

Living people are named by first name only. Photographs are openly licensed or public-domain pictures of places, not family portraits. City history is context, not a claim about what any one person in the family did.
